Before you come

Quick answers.

Are you open right now?

Mon to Fri 9 AM to 8 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 6 PM, closed Sunday. The header on the home page shows live open or closed status.

Do you take walk-ins?

Every open hour. Weekends fill by mid-morning, so book ahead Friday and Saturday.

Where do I park?

Two-hour street parking on Alder and Vine, free after 6 PM. The lot behind the bakery is private — it does get ticketed.

Do you cut hair for large groups?

Weddings, teams and shoots, yes. Message us with the date and headcount and we will price the block.
