Four chairs, four hands.

Request anyone by name when you book. If your regular is off that day, we will say so before you commit.

Rosa Delgado

Owner · Master barber

Rosa opened the shop in 2014 with one chair, a secondhand mirror and a rule she still enforces: nobody leaves the chair unhappy. She trained in classic barbering and spent six years on a salon floor before that, which is the whole reason this place cuts both ways.

  • Scissor cuts
  • Grey coverage
  • Consultations

Tue – Sat

Theo Amara

Barber · Fades

Theo does the sharpest skin fade in the district and knows it. Ask for a number and he will ask you three questions first — hair growth pattern, how often you come back, and whether you actually use product. The answers change the cut.

  • Skin fades
  • Line-ups
  • Kids cuts

Mon – Fri

Marisol Vega

Stylist · Colour & treatments

Marisol runs the salon side: grey coverage that does not announce itself, keratin smoothing, and the scalp massage people book on its own. If you are unsure whether colour is worth it, she will talk you out of it as readily as into it.

  • Colour
  • Keratin
  • Women’s short cuts

Wed – Sat

Sam Okafor

Barber · Straight razor

Sam took over the razor bench in 2019 and has not rushed a shave since. Hot towel, three passes, cold finish. He also keeps the shop’s blades sharp, which is why nobody else is allowed to touch the strop.

  • Hot towel shaves
  • Beard sculpting
  • Classic cuts

Mon, Thu – Sat
