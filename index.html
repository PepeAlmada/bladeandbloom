First visit

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in and we will fit you around the book. On Fridays and Saturdays an appointment is the safer bet — those days run full.

How long should I set aside?

A buzz is fifteen minutes. A fade with a beard trim is closer to fifty. Package bookings run an hour and change.

Do you cut women’s hair?

Short styles, yes — scissor and clipper work with styling. For long-hair cutting and full colour services, call and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right shop.
